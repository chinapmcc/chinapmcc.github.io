Project Management Professional (PMP)

Demonstrate your ability to lead projects in any industry with this globally recognized certification. 通过这项全球公认的认证，证明您有能力领导任何行业的项目。

什么是PMP认证？

The PMP acknowledges candidates skilled at managing the people, processes, and business priorities of professional projects. PMI, the world’s leading authority on project management, created the PMP to recognize project managers who have proven they have project leadership experience and expertise in any way of working.
PMP 认可擅长管理专业项目的人员、流程和业务优先事项的候选人。PMI 是世界领先的项目管理权威机构，它创建了 PMP，以表彰那些证明自己在任何工作方式上都具有项目领导经验和专业知识的项目经理。

To obtain PMP certification, a project manager must meet certain requirements and then pass a 180-question exam. The PMP exam was created by project leaders for project leaders, so each test question can be related to real-life project management experiences.
要获得 PMP 认证，项目经理必须满足某些要求，然后通过 180 道题的考试。PMP考试是由项目负责人为项目负责人创建的，因此每个测试问题都可以与现实生活中的项目管理经验相关。

The PMP Exam is available in the following languages: Arabic, Chinese (Simplified + Traditional), English, French, German, Hebrew, Indonesian, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Polish (Poland), Portuguese (Brazilian), Russian, Spanish (Mexico + Spain), and Turkish.
PMP 考试提供以下语言版本：阿拉伯语、中文（简体 + 繁体）、英语、法语、德语、希伯来语、印度尼西亚语、意大利语、日语、韩语、波兰语（波兰）、葡萄牙语（巴西）、俄语、西班牙语（墨西哥 + 西班牙）和土耳其语。

PMP认证证明您在以下方面具有很高的技能：

  • Motivating people and teams through all phases of a successful project. 在成功项目的各个阶段激励人员和团队。

  • Using predictive, agile and hybrid approaches to determine which way of working is best for each project. 使用预测性、敏捷性和混合性方法来确定哪种工作方式最适合每个项目。

  • Highlighting the success of a project and its impact on overall strategic organizational goals. 突出项目的成功及其对整体战略组织目标的影响。

为什么要获得PMP认证？

The PMP is ranked as the top project management certification in North America by CIO Magazine because it demonstrates that you have the specific skills employers seek, a dedication to excellence, and the capacity to perform at the highest levels.

PMP 被 CIO 杂志评为北美顶级项目管理认证，因为它表明您拥有雇主寻求的特定技能、追求卓越的奉献精神以及执行最高水平的能力。

In fact, research indicates that employers will need to fill nearly 2.3 million new project-oriented roles each year through 2030.

事实上，研究表明，到 2030 年，雇主每年将需要填补近 230 万个新的项目导向职位。

With demand for project managers at an all-time high, there has never been a better time to earn your PMP certification.

随着对项目经理的需求空前高涨，现在是获得 PMP 认证的最佳时机。

9000万人 2500万人 1200万人
Project management employees currently in project-based industries across the world. New project management professionals needed to meet global talent demands by 2030. New employees driven by expansion, and a growth of 13 million by retirements.
目前在全球以项目为基础的行业中的项目管理员工。 到 2030 年，需要新的项目管理专业人员来满足全球人才需求。 扩张推动了新员工，退休人数增长了 1300 万。

我有资格参加PMP考试吗？

获得 PMP 认证是一项承诺。这是它有价值的部分原因。如果你有实际的项目管理经验，你已经完成了最难的部分。在申请之前，请确保您满足以下PMP认证要求之一：

  • Four-Year College / University Degree 四年制学院/大学学位

  • 36 months of experience leading projects within the past eight years 36 个月在过去八年内领导项目的经验

  • 35 hours of project management education/training or CAPM(®) certification 35小时的项目管理教育/培训或CAPM (®) 认证

或者

  • High School or Secondary School Diploma 高中或中学文凭

  • 60 months of experience leading projects within the past eight years 在过去八年内有60个月的项目领导经验

  • 35 hours of project management education/training or CAPM(®) certification 35小时的项目管理教育/培训或CAPM (®) 认证
